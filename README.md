## 📌 Project Overview

Sales leaders rely on timely, accurate, and actionable information to monitor business performance, evaluate sales effectiveness, and make strategic decisions.

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance & Revenue Intelligence Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** and **Excel** to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key sales metrics, evaluate product and regional performance, identify sales trends, measure progress against targets, and support evidence-based commercial decision-making.

Rather than reporting sales figures alone, the solution provides a holistic view of business performance through interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, and analytical insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

As organizations grow, sales data is often spread across multiple reports and spreadsheets, making it difficult for management to answer critical commercial questions in a timely manner.

Examples include:

- Are we achieving our sales targets?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions consistently outperform expectations?
- Which product categories require additional attention?
- Are sales improving or declining over time?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- How should management prioritize future sales efforts?

Without centralized reporting, identifying performance gaps and growth opportunities becomes increasingly difficult.

This project addresses these challenges by consolidating sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

---

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall sales performance through interactive dashboards.

# 📂 Dataset Description

This project is based on a structured sales dataset representing transactional sales activities across multiple products, customer segments, sales regions, and time periods.

The dataset was prepared to simulate a real-world commercial reporting environment where management requires reliable and timely information to monitor sales performance and support strategic decision-making.

The data was imported into Power BI from Microsoft Excel and transformed into a structured analytical model suitable for interactive business intelligence reporting.

---

- Customer Orders
- Sales Revenue

- Sales Regions

These dimensions enable multidimensional analysis of commercial performance.

# 🔄 Data Transformation

Several transformations were applied before loading the dataset into Power BI.

Examples include:

- Standardized date formats
- Created Year, Quarter, Month, and Month Number fields
- Categorized products into business groups
- Organized sales regions
- Prepared measures for KPI calculations

These transformations simplify reporting and improve dashboard performance.

---

# 🗄 Data Modeling

A star-schema-inspired model was used to support efficient reporting.

```text
                 Date
                  │
                  │
Region ─── Sales Fact ─── Product
                  │
                  │
             Customer
```

The Sales Fact table stores transactional information, while supporting dimensions provide descriptive attributes for slicing and filtering the data.

This approach improves scalability, simplifies report design, and follows common business intelligence modeling practices.

# 📊 KPI Framework

The dashboard focuses on commercial performance indicators commonly monitored by sales leadership teams.

---

## Total Revenue

Measures the total monetary value of all completed sales.

**Business Value**

Provides an overall measure of company sales performance.

---

## Total Orders

Counts the total number of completed customer orders.

**Business Value**

Helps evaluate sales activity and customer demand.

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

Measures the average revenue generated per order.

**Formula**

```text
Average Order Value =
Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders
```

**Business Value**

Supports pricing strategy and customer purchasing analysis.

---

## Sales Growth

Measures the change in sales over time.

**Business Value**

Identifies periods of business growth or decline.

---

## Sales Target Achievement

Compares actual sales against predefined business targets.

**Business Value**

Evaluates whether commercial objectives are being achieved.

---

## Revenue by Product

Measures revenue generated by each product.

**Business Value**

Identifies top-performing products and opportunities for product portfolio optimization.

---

## Revenue by Region

Measures sales performance across geographical regions.

**Business Value**

Supports regional planning and territory management.

---

## Customer Contribution

Measures revenue generated by individual customers.

**Business Value**

Identifies high-value customers and supports customer relationship management.

---

## Monthly Sales Trend

Tracks sales performance over time.

**Business Value**

Highlights seasonality, growth trends, and unusual performance fluctuations.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis generated several valuable commercial insights.

---

## Insight 1 – Revenue Distribution

Revenue is not evenly distributed across products, customers, or regions.

A relatively small proportion of products and customers contributes a significant share of total sales, highlighting opportunities for focused sales and marketing strategies.

---

## Insight 2 – Product Performance

High sales volume does not always indicate strong business performance.

Products generating high revenue should also be evaluated for profitability, pricing strategy, and long-term demand.

---

## Insight 3 – Regional Performance

Sales performance varies considerably across regions.

Understanding regional differences helps management allocate resources more effectively and identify markets with the greatest growth potential.

---

## Insight 4 – Customer Contribution

Customer purchasing behaviour differs significantly.

Identifying high-value customers enables organizations to prioritize retention efforts and strengthen long-term customer relationships.

---

## Insight 5 – Sales Trends

Sales fluctuate over time due to seasonality, customer demand, promotions, and market conditions.

Continuous monitoring enables proactive business planning rather than reactive decision-making.
